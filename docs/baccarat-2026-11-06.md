# Баккара Mini App

## Rules

Punto Banco, player versus banker, with a cryptographically shuffled 8-deck shoe generated server-side for each hand. Aces count as one; 2–9 score face value; 10/J/Q/K count zero. Totals use the last digit. Natural 8 or 9 ends the deal. The player draws on 0–5; the banker follows the standard third-card tableau. There is no player action during the deal.

Wagers: Player pays 1:1 net (gross ×2), Banker pays 0.95:1 net after 5% commission (gross ×1.95), Tie pays 8:1 net (gross ×9). A tie pushes Player and Banker bets; a Tie wager loses on every non-tie result.

The player sees the cards, totals and result after the server has dealt the completed hand. The animation reveals those cards only; it does not determine or alter the outcome.
